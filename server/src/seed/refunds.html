This Refund Policy applies to subscriptions and credit packs purchased from Eligoo AI ("Eligoo", "we", "us") for the Eligoo platform. Payments are processed by our merchant of record, Dodo Payments, and refunds are returned through the same payment method used for the purchase.

1. Try before you pay

Every new workspace starts with a 7-day free trial and no payment card is required. We encourage you to use the trial to confirm that Eligoo fits your business before choosing a paid plan.

2. Subscriptions

  • 7-day refund window. If you subscribe to a paid plan and have not used it, you may request a full refund within 7 days of the charge. "Not used" means no credits have been consumed by AI employees during that billing period.
  • Partial use. If credits have been consumed within the 7-day window, we may at our discretion issue a pro-rated refund for the unused part of the period after deducting the value of credits used.
  • After 7 days. Charges older than 7 days are not refundable. You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • Renewals. Renewal charges follow the same 7-day rule, counted from the renewal date. To avoid an unwanted renewal, cancel before the end of the current period.

3. Credit packs

Credit packs are one-time purchases. A pack is refundable within 7 days of purchase if none of its credits have been used. Once any credit from a pack has been consumed the pack is non-refundable.

4. Service failures

If a documented platform fault prevented your AI employees from working, we will restore the credits affected or, where appropriate, refund the affected period, regardless of the 7-day window. Contact support with the details.

5. How to request a refund

Email billing@eligoo.in from your account email address with your workspace name and the invoice number. We respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued by Dodo Payments and normally appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank; most billing issues can be resolved directly. Workspaces with an open chargeback may be paused until the dispute is settled.

7. Taxes and currency

Refunds are made in the currency of the original charge. Any taxes collected on the refunded amount are refunded with it. We are not responsible for exchange-rate differences or bank fees.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes apply to purchases made after the date they are posted here.
