This Privacy Policy explains how Eligoo AI ("Eligoo", "we", "us"), a company incorporated in India, collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit eligoo.in, create a workspace or use the Eligoo platform (the "Service"). It applies alongside our Terms of Service.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data. Name, email address, company name and password or sign-in provider details when you sign up, and the plan and billing status of your workspace.
  • Billing data. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. We receive confirmation of payment, invoice details and the last digits of a card for display; full card numbers are never stored by Eligoo.
  • Workspace content. The business information, brand materials, goals, instructions and files you give your AI employees, and the work they produce.
  • Connected-account data. When you connect a third-party service (for example email, advertising, social or CRM accounts), we store the access tokens and the data those services return that is needed for the tasks you configure. You can disconnect any account at any time.
  • Usage data. Actions taken in the workspace, credits consumed, approval decisions, device and browser information, IP address and approximate location, and pages visited on eligoo.in.
  • Communications. Messages you send to support and, where an AI employee places calls or joins meetings on your behalf, transcripts and recordings of those interactions, subject to the consent you obtain from participants.

2. How we use data

We use personal data to provide and operate the Service; to run AI employees on the tasks you configure; to process payments and manage subscriptions and credits; to secure accounts and prevent abuse; to provide support; to send service notices and, where you have not opted out, product updates; to analyse usage so we can improve the Service; and to comply with legal obligations.

3. AI processing

Your workspace content is processed by large language models and other AI providers to produce the work you request. We contract with these providers on terms that prohibit using your content to train their general models. We do not use your Customer Data to train models shared with other customers.

4. Sharing

We share personal data only with: service providers who host and operate the Service (cloud infrastructure, AI model providers, email delivery, telephony, analytics and error monitoring); Dodo Payments for billing; the third-party services you choose to connect; professional advisers; and authorities where required by law. Each provider is bound by contractual obligations of confidentiality and security. We do not sell personal data.

5. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect against fraud, and first-party analytics to understand how the site is used. If you arrive via a partner link, a cookie remembers that referral for 90 days so the partner can be credited. You can control cookies in your browser settings; disabling necessary cookies may prevent sign-in.

6. International transfers

Eligoo is based in India, and our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where data is transferred across borders we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, and we apply the same protections wherever data is processed.

7. Retention

We keep account and workspace data for as long as your workspace is active. When a workspace is closed, we delete or anonymise its data within 30 days, except for billing records, which we retain for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and for limited backup copies, which expire on their normal rotation.

8. Your rights and data deletion

You can access and update your account details in the workspace at any time. You may also ask us to provide a copy of your personal data, correct it, restrict its processing, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, or delete it. To delete your workspace and all associated data, email privacy@eligoo.in from your account email or use the deletion option in your workspace settings; we will confirm within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days. Rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the GDPR and similar laws apply according to your location. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, isolated per-workspace data, audit logging and regular reviews. No system is completely secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post changes here and notify you by email or in the workspace when they are material.
